Research Area: Computational Genomics & Interface Design

My research focuses on the intersection of computational genomics and software optimization. I am particularly interested in using C++ to build high-performance algorithms for analyzing large-scale genomic sequences and identifying biological patterns.

Additionally, I explore Human-Computer Interaction (HCI) within scientific software. By applying UI/UX principles and prototyping tools like Figma, I research ways to make complex bioinformatics data more accessible and intuitive for the scientific community.

Research Focus

  • Genome algorithm efficiency
  • Scientific UX design
  • Data-driven visualization
